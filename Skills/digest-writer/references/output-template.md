# Weekly Digest Post Template

Fill this in and write it to `Content/Weekly-Industry-Digest/<niche-slug>/<run_date>.md`.

```markdown
# Weekly Industry Digest: <niche> (<run_date>)

## LinkedIn Post (copy-paste ready)

<Hook line, one punchy sentence.>

<Highlight bullet 1: fact + why it matters for a founder>
<Highlight bullet 2: fact + why it matters for a founder>
<Highlight bullet 3: fact + why it matters for a founder>
<Highlight bullet 4 (optional)>
<Highlight bullet 5 (optional)>
<Highlight bullet 6 (optional)>

<Closing discussion question or opinion prompt.>

## Sources
- News scan: `Content/Weekly-Industry-Digest/<niche-slug>/<run_date>/news-scan.md`
- Funding & launches: `Content/Weekly-Industry-Digest/<niche-slug>/<run_date>/funding-launches.md`
```

Formatting rules for the "LinkedIn Post" section (this is the part the user copies straight into LinkedIn):
- Keep each bullet to 1-2 short lines. LinkedIn readers skim, don't write paragraphs.
- No em dashes anywhere in the post. Use commas or periods instead.
- No hashtags unless the user's `config/digest-topic.md` or a prior instruction explicitly asks for them.
- Total post length: aim for 120-200 words so it reads as a real weekly digest, not a wall of text.
